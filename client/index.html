About — Λίγα λόγια

I photograph the way memory actually feels.

I grew up between Athens and a small village on the slopes of Pelion, which is probably why I keep coming back to slow afternoons, warm stone, and the people who sit inside them.

For the last decade I’ve worked with couples, families and magazines who want photographs that breathe — unhurried, unposed, and made on real film whenever the day allows it.
